About Me

I'm a Master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Portland State University, specializing in Embedded Systems, Firmware, and Hardware–Software co-design. I enjoy building systems from bare metal to Linux and validating them through simulation, emulation, and real hardware.

Technical Interests

My work focuses on the intersection of hardware and software, where I can apply both theoretical knowledge and hands-on skills. I'm particularly interested in:

  • Embedded Linux bring-up and device driver development
  • FPGA-based system design and verification
  • Real-time operating systems (RTOS)
  • Hardware-software co-design and optimization
  • Peripheral interfacing and communication protocols
